The procedural pass

MCAR, MAR, MNAR and the missingness matrix

Source: Chapter 3, “Missingness”; Rubin 1976.

4,000 people with income rising with age. MCAR drops 30% at random; MAR drops 60% of under-30s and 15% of others; MNAR drops 70% of the top third and 10% of the rest. Rules are mine; the taxonomy is Rubin (1976) as the chapter cites it.
