Definitions and the theorem

The impossibility theorem and the threshold

Source: Chapter 7, “The arithmetic”; docs/Exam terms - compact list (threshold).

Steps 1 and 2 are the chapter algebra with its numbers. Steps 3 and 4 use a stated illustrative score model: group A scores follow Beta(3,2) (mean 0.6), group B Beta(1.5,3.5) (mean 0.3), and the outcome is 1 with probability equal to the score, so scores are calibrated in both groups. Rates are computed by integrating the densities above the cutoff. The cutoff for B that matches the false-positive rate is found by search.
