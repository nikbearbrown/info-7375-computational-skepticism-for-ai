Why the gap is real

Induction, complementarity and the boundary

Source: Chapter 13, “Three categorical limits”; Exam terms, full inventory, Chapter 13.

Covered and excluded shares are computed from the sample square: s squared over 440 squared. The boundary length is 4s. Hume, An Enquiry Concerning Human Understanding, 1748, section IV.

Why the gap is real

Mediocristan, Extremistan, turkey, black swan

Source: Chapter 13, “Limit 3: The data-world gap”; Exam terms, full inventory, Chapter 13.

1,000 seeded draws: normal heights, and a Pareto tail with exponent 1.1. The turkey curve is the rule of succession, (days+1)/(days+2). Taleb, The Black Swan, 2007.

Tests and arguments

Validation set, distribution shift, wrong tests

Source: Chapter 13, “The model that passed every test”; Exam terms, shortlist (validation set), compact list (distribution shift).

Two unit-variance classes 3.1 apart with the threshold midway give 93.9%, the chapter’s 94% as a computed example. Accuracy under a shift of both classes is exact from the normal CDF. Validation set and distribution shift are glossary rows; the chapter names them without explaining.
