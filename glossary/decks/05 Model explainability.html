Source: Chapter 5, “The four axioms”.

Computing it

Sampling, TreeSHAP, correlated features, conditional sampling

Source: Chapter 5, “Computational shortcuts” and “The correlated feature problem”; Aas, Jullum and Løland 2021.

Sampling draws from the chapter’s six orderings of income with a seeded generator. The tree is drawn to depth 3, so 2 to the power 3 leaves. The scatter uses correlation 0.9 and 140 seeded points; off the band means more than two residual standard deviations from the line. The chapter calls the sampling estimator Monte Carlo sampling (KernelSHAP).
