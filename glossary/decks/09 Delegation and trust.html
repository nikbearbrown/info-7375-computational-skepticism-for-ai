Trust and the record

Reliance should match reliability

Source: Chapter 9, “Trust calibration”; Exercise 7.

Steps 1, 2 and 4 are illustrative. Step 3 uses the chapter exercise 7 numbers: 186 accepted, 14 overridden of 200; 6 errors in a 40-case audit. The 95% Wilson interval for 6 of 40 is computed here, not in the chapter, and is wide: the 7% override rate sits at its lower edge.
