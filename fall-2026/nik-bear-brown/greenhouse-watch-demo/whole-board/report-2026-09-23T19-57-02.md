**Why read it.** It is the shortest honest list of what is new and plausibly relevant at figma today, with the reason each item is on it. The decision — whether any of these is worth a day of your life — is yours and has not been made for you.

**What it did not do.** It did not judge fit. Every item below is a word-for-word match between the résumé and the posting; a high score means many words matched, not that the job is good for you. Read the posting before deciding anything.
