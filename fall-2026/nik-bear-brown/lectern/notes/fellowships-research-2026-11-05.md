## What this says about the list

- The three programs with an official page read (Anthropic, Constellation, 0G) match the post's dates.
- The post is a mix of kinds: a research residency, a postdoc, two founder programs, an equity accelerator and a school for young builders. They share a deadline month, not a kind of opportunity.
- The Horowitz Andreessen Academy is for people finishing high school and the CMU postdoc needs a recent PhD, so they are unlikely to be wanted by a watch aimed at an established academic.

## What a fellowships search would look like (not built)

A fellowship is not a job posting: it has a program page, an application window and a deadline, so "new or changed" means a window opening, a date moving or a cohort closing. That does not fit the board readers (Greenhouse, Workday, Oracle HCM) that the consulting and federal-508 channels use. The Anthropic residency is itself posted on a Greenhouse board (US job 5438311008), so the existing Anthropic entry would already see it as a job posting.
