# Targets: every company, school and research group Lectern watches, and where its data comes from

## Executive summary

**What this is.** The list of everything the job search and the research search are pointed at: companies, business schools, research institutes, hospitals, think tanks, a biotech, and the public databases behind them. Each is classified by what it is, with the direct link to where its data lives, the script that reads it (or a plain "none"), and what is still missing. **Why read it.** A target is only real if there is a way to read it. The project had grown a watch list in one file, a school list in another and a research-places list in a third, and nobody could see at a glance which targets are actually being read and which are only intentions. **What it found.** Of the targets with a reader: 6 company job boards are read; Google is collected but not filtered; Microsoft has never been read (it throttled), Amazon and Meta have never run here; 21 business schools are covered only through two shared job-feed searches, because most schools' own job systems cannot be read by script. Of the research places, **only one early reader exists** (`collect/collectors/biolabs.py`, built 2026-10-20 from the Biojobs repository, run on four directory pages): it finds lab links and scores each lab page for data-heavy biology, but no lab has yet been read for fit. Two targets turned out to have readable job feeds that Lectern has no reader for (Moderna on Workday, Hugging Face on Workable). **Added 2026-10-28:** section H, 23 game studios and engine makers reviewed one by one for a second Discord channel (10 on the list, 7 dropped with a reason, 6 not readable yet); they are read only through the ai-jobs feed, never by `collect.py`. **Added 2026-10-28, later:** section I, 72 consulting firms and look-alikes researched for a third channel (5 on the list, 43 to do, 24 tagged and dropped); the five are read by `collect/consulting_collect.py`, never by `collect.py`.

**The tables below are generated from `config/targets.json`** (run `python3 ops/build_from_targets.py` after editing it; `--check` says whether anything differs). Edit the data there, never the tables here. The prose around them is written by hand.

Links were checked with a plain request on 2026-10-20 (HTTP status shown in the notes where it was not 200). A page that answers a script is not the same as a page a script may read: check the site's robots file before adding a reader.

## How targets are classified

| Type | Meaning | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **AI lab with an education product** | Builds AI and sells or runs an education product | Anthropic, OpenAI |
| **Tool company with an education program** | A product company with a real education arm (an academy, a university, an education edition) | Canva, Figma, Notion |
| **Developer-advocacy employer** | Known for paying people to teach developers | none watched now (GitLab, Twilio, Netlify and Supabase were removed 2026-10-20) |
| **Tool company, no education program yet** | Watched cheaply; the education push may come | Miro |
| **Big technology company with an education footprint** | Large incumbents with education businesses | Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Meta, Adobe, Salesforce, GitHub, Shopify |
| **University business school** | Where faculty listings about AI appear | the 21 schools below |
| **University research unit** | A department, center or lab inside a university | Harvard Medical School's Division of Nutrition, Northeastern's AI hub |
| **Research institute or think tank** | Professor Bear's broad sense: smart people on hard problems, whatever the kind (science institute, economics center, policy shop) | the Broad, the Whitehead, NBER |
| **Hospital research** | Research arms of hospitals | Boston Children's, Mass General |
| **Biotech company** | A company that does biology research for profit | Moderna |
| **Game studio or engine maker** | Makes games or a game engine; judged only for the games channel, never read by `collect.py` for #professor-bear | the 10 studios on the list in section H |
| **Consulting firm** | Sells consulting; judged only for the consulting channel, never read by `collect.py` for #professor-bear | the 5 boards on the list in section I |
| **Company that is not a consulting firm; in-house strategy group** | Named in the consulting list but an engineering, IT-services, insurance or data company, or a company's own strategy team; tagged, not treated as consulting | the 24 tagged in section I |
| **Data source (not a target)** | A database the targets are read through | NIH RePORTER, NSF Award Search |

"Why it is a target" is taken from the reason recorded in the watch list or from Professor Bear's own words; where the repository gives none, the table says so. The classification is a reading, not a fact about the target.

## E. Research places (the part-time research search)

There is **one early reader**, `collect/collectors/biolabs.py` (seeds and rules in `config/biolabs.json`, adapted from the Biojobs repository): it checks `robots.txt`, says what it is in its User-Agent, finds lab links on a directory page, scores each lab page for data-heavy biology words and writes a local record. Run on 2026-10-20 on four directory pages: Harvard Medical School's Division of Nutrition people page, the Chan School's biostatistics page and Boston Children's labs page produced candidate lab links (the Chan page first returned degree programmes, so the filter was tightened); **the Harvard Medical School labs page answers 200 to `curl` but 404 to the reader, unresolved**; and Broad, Dana-Farber, MIT Biology and Wyss directory pages are unreadable (403 or 404). Everything else was read by hand on 2026-10-19. The design for a reader is in `lab-outreach/LAB-RECORD.md`: three stages (a department directory gives the principal investigators, each person's profile gives the lab, the lab site gives what it does and who is in it). Funding is checked at the institution level with `check-awards.py` against NIH RePORTER and the NSF Award Search (it was run for six institutions on 2026-10-19; it lives in the Prompt Engineering repository's `mode-build-assignment/scripts/funding/`, not in the master folder). Records that name people stay local.
