- Slide 7 (LIME), step 1: the picture description in the render function says 'a curve with ten perturbed points', but the code, the speaker note and the still all use six perturbed points.

The slide 11 note says its charts 'reuse numbers computed on earlier slides', but these twenty slopes are not on any earlier slide.

- Slide 5 step 1: the heading says 'FORCE PLOT: EACH ARROW PUSHES FROM THE AVERAGE' and the picture description says 'three arrows', but the drawing is three plain blocks with no arrowheads.
- Slide 5 step 3 names the dummy feature 'feature D', while slide 4 uses D for debt-to-income; a viewer may take the zero bar to be debt-to-income (which is +0.062).
- Slide 4 step 4: the bar labels '+0.08' and '+0.09' are drawn across the red dashed average line and are hard to make out.
- Slide 3 carries one badge, Simulation (seeded), for all four steps, but steps 1 to 3 show the hypothetical loan numbers from the text and only step 4 (the two scatter plots) is a seeded simulation. Slide 10 is badged Conceptual illustration yet pair 2 shows the Exercise A5 values (0.19, 0.31, 0.04, 0.46).

- The deck and chapter never expand the acronyms SHAP and LIME. The narration gives the standard expansions (Shapley additive explanations, local interpretable model-agnostic explanations) because the brief requires an expansion; these words are not on any slide.
- Slide 9: per the speaker note, the provider-side details of the agent case are marked in the chapter as still to be verified against the primary source; the narration says 'as this course tells the case' and does not present them as checked.
