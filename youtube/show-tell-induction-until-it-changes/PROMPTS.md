**Your Turn (BHTF)**, shown and read in full (spoken with "describe it" for `[describe it]`):

```
Here is a pattern my team or model relies on: [describe it]. What process in the world produces this pattern? What would have to change in that process for the pattern to stop holding? For each change, name one signal I could watch that would tell me it had happened.
```
