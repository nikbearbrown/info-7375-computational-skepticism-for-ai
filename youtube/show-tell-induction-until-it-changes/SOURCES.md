# SOURCES — show-tell-induction-until-it-changes

**What this is.** Every source the film draws on, with where the local copy lives. **Why.** The film states Hume's position in his own words and one external data set (the US Census Bureau's quarterly e-commerce share of retail sales, Bear's COVID example); each is checked against its primary, fetched raw on 2026-09-27, not against the chapter or a summary. **Found.** Hume's text supports both halves of the film: no argument proves the future will resemble the past (IV.ii), and yet "none but a fool or madman" disputes experience, and custom is "the great guide of human life" (IV.ii ¶31, V.i ¶36). The Census data show ten years of small, steady quarterly steps and then a jump of about four points in 2Q2020; the release figures (11.8 → 16.1) differ slightly from today's revised series (11.9 → 16.3), so the film rounds to "about twelve … about sixteen".
