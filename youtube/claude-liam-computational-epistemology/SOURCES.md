   schemes, eval platforms, "as of mid-2026" claims) will date the episode;
   the mechanisms they implement are already taught by the surviving acts.
   Deep-explainer hard rule 4.
2. **The source's own caveat honored.** The source document flags that its
   lineage mixes verified formalism with fabricated-looking benchmark
   numbers (its final "note on source quality"). No specific accuracy
   percentages, correlation values, or benchmark figures from the source
   appear on screen or in narration. The 90%/60% calibration pair in B17/B37
   is the source's own illustrative example and is presented as
   illustrative, not empirical.
3. **The lineage-confounder marginalization formula** (Part 3) is spoken
   conceptually in B25 but kept off screen — one idea per beat; the DAG
   carries it.
4. **"Interpretability work keeps finding that gap"** (B16) is kept soft and
   unquantified, matching the source's own hedged phrasing about
   chain-of-thought faithfulness.
