Knowing What's True in the Age of AI — Computational Epistemology

Philosophy's three oldest questions — what is knowledge, how is it justified, what do we do about doubt — rebuilt as working verification code. Justified true belief and the Gettier problem become an entailment check that catches right-answer-wrong-path; internalism versus externalism becomes track records over self-reports; foundationalism and coherentism become a source anchor plus a consensus check discounted for shared training lineage; Descartes' demon and Hume's induction become the actual threat model for synthetic media; and Bayesian epistemology closes it out as the one place the old subject and the new code were always the same discipline.
