## Source-integrity note (the load-bearing caveat)
The chapter is unusually candid that its tier taxonomy (**Irreducibly Human**) is the author's own **forthcoming, as-yet-unpublished** work and "**you cannot check that source.**" The chapter itself instructs: treat the tier labels as scaffolding; the claim that does *not* depend on the taxonomy is the verifiable one — **responsibility distributes, and the distribution must be closed by design** (Matthias; Santoni de Sio & Mecacci). This cut follows that instruction exactly: the spine (distribution, structural need for a human, the attestation, the governance counterfactual, answerability) stands on the checkable sources; the seven tiers appear as the chapter's own framing device, never as independently established fact. No tier *numbers* or invented capacities were added.
