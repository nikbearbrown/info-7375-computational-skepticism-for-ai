Recap
What we established
  • A dashboard's layout argues before anyone takes in a number.
  • State the question first, then pick position or length for quantity.
  • Aleatoric is chance in the data, epistemic is doubt about the data.
  • Label every error bar, and draw uncertainty at equal visual weight.
  • Truncated, inverted and inconsistent axes change the story, not the data.
  • An evidence-grade chart shows what could prove the claim wrong.
