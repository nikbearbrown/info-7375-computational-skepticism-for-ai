# SOURCES — show-tell-doubt-what-matters

**What this is.** Every source the film draws on, with where the local copy lives. **Why.** The film says what Descartes wrote in three works; each is checked against his own text (the Latin first edition of the *Meditations*, and John Veitch's public-domain English translations), fetched raw with curl on 2026-09-27, not against the chapter or a summary. **Found.** The texts support everything the film attributes to Descartes: doubt "once in my life", safe because "the end I now seek is not action but knowledge"; the house you need while rebuilding yours; the provisory code; the lost traveller who walks straight; act on "what is most probable". They do NOT support risk-weighted doubt: that is Bear's rule, and the film keeps it his.
