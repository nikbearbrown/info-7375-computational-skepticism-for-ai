The Test You Did Not Design.

A clinical decision-support system passed every test its engineers designed — 94% accuracy, every review threshold, regulatory clearance, fairness within tolerance — and still harmed three patients. Liam, in for Bear, takes apart why: three limits that scaling cannot fix (meaning, intentionality, and the gap between data and world), what Turing and Searle actually settle, where the limits bite, why the override has to be real, and why the authority to refuse is the most important authority in the system.

Based on Nik Bear Brown's essay "The Limits of AI: What the Tools Cannot Do — The Test You Did Not Design" (skepticism.ai, Apr 28, 2026).
