Act two, three limits, not problems waiting on a bigger model, but features of what these systems are. Meaning intentionality, the gap between data and world. The symbols have reference in the world. The system has no representation of the reference, it manipulates the symbols, and the meaning is supplied by whoever reads the output. You can hear the objection, large multimodal models pick up something like meaning from embeddings and grounding. It's a serious objection and it doesn't need settling. The second limit is intentionality, the philosopher's word for aboutness. When you say the kettle is on, your words point at a particular kettle in a particular kitchen. It's aboutness tracks the user's reading, not a directiveness of its own. So someone has to supply the pointing and that someone is the human supervisor. The third limit is the one I most certain of.
