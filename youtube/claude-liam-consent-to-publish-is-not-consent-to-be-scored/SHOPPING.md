## Vox-run constraints (these bind the sourcing)

- **R1 = B03 + B04.** One plate, two crops. Shoot the screenshot at full page width so
  the tight crop (the date line) and the wide crop (the controls) come from the *same*
  image. Two different screenshots will break the handoff.
- **R2 = B22 + B23.** Same rule: one document, tight crop then wide.

## The hard constraint on B26

The beat's claim is that scores get redistributed. The still must therefore show a score
**that its own author chose to publish** — a writer embedding their own Pangram result as
argument in the controversy.

Do **not** use a screenshot of someone else's score republished without their consent.
Illustrating a privacy harm by committing it is the one move this film cannot make. If a
self-published example can't be found, **cut B26 and drop the redistribution point** — the
argument is not worth the contradiction. `FACTCHECK.md` §C carries this as a build gate.

## Not needed

- No portraits of living lawyers, judges, or Substack/Pangram staff. Nothing in the
  script requires a face, and a legal analysis illustrated with people's faces invites
  exactly the personalisation the film is arguing against.
- No AI-generated stills. Every slot above is either a screenshot Bear takes, a licensed
  archive image, or a synthetic mock with no real person's data.

## Worst case if nothing is sourced

The film still compiles and is watchable: 7 slates out of 36 beats, all 10 Manim and 10
Remotion beats render for real, audio is real (Kokoro `am_onyx`, free). That is the Gate
D1 previz — **never present it as a finished cut.**
