## Things noticed on the deck itself (not changed; for Bear)

- Slide 5 (Cohen's kappa), step 2 caption: 'Unblind one coder and agreement falls to 0.20.' It is kappa that falls to 0.20. Raw agreement (Po) falls from 0.80 to 0.60, as the table itself shows.
- Slide 2 (Estimator), step 2: the caption says the filtered estimates 'centre near 14%' and the bias arrow starts at 14.3%, but in this seeded draw the 24 red dots at n = 50 average 16.0% (recomputed from the deck's own rng), so the faint red mean tick sits visibly right of the arrow's start. At n = 2,000 the mean is 14.5% and the picture agrees.
- One fairness criterion carries four names: 'Equal FPR and FNR' (slides 1 and 10), its alias 'Equalized odds' (slide 10), 'Equal error rates' (slide 11 pair 3 and the slide 10 caption) and 'equal TPR and FPR' (slide 12). The exam term is the first; the narration ties the others to it.
- Slide 10, Impossibility theorem: 'With unequal base rates, not all three can hold at once.' The slide names only two things (Calibration parity, and Equal FPR and FNR as one term), so 'all three' has no visible referent. It means calibration parity, equal FPR and equal FNR.
- Slide 10, step 1: both panels show identical bars for both groups (60/60, 10/10, 65/65), which reads as if calibration parity and equal error rates hold together. With the 30% and 50% base rates used from step 2 on, they cannot. The picture is each definition on its own.
- Badges versus notes: slides 3, 5, 8, 10 and 12 are badged 'Computed', but the notes say their inputs are illustrative (patient counts, coder tables, data-entry shares, the approval model, TPR 35% and FPR 10%). The narration says 'made-up' for each.
- Slide 4 is badged 'Simulation (seeded)', but the voting grid in step 2 (60 voted, 75 say they voted) is not a simulation and is not labelled illustrative on the slide or in the note. Narrated as a made-up poll.
- Slide 12, step 5: the chart labels the paths 'via sample: 2', 'via model: 3', 'via reviewer: 45'. On slide 9 the sample path also runs through the model ('Two routes run through the model'), and the 3 is the proxy-into-model path. 'via proxy into model' would match the graph.
- Slide 3, step 3: 'Stable: 630 kept x 1.11 = 700'. 630 x 1.11 is 699.3; the exact weight is 1/0.9 = 1.111. Rounding only.
- Slide 1: the group 'Ten ways in' lists thirteen terms (the ten bias types plus three tools: inverse probability weighting, funnel plot, Cohen's kappa).
- On-screen chapter references that the film narration is not allowed to speak: 'opens in Chapter 4, closes in Chapter 12' and '(Chapter 12)' on slide 8 step 1, and '33 terms from Chapter 6' on slide 1.
