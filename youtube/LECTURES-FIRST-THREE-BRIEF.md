## How to use the sources
- **The deck gives the lecture's shape and Bear's teaching order; the updated chapter gives the content.** Where they differ, the chapter wins and `ACTS.md` records the difference in a "DECK vs CHAPTER" table (what the deck had that the chapter no longer says; what the chapter has that the deck never had). The deck's own figures and charts are candidate material: rebuild them as moving beats, never paste.
- **The updated chapters carry unresolved merge-conflict blocks. Never edit the chapter files, and never silently resolve a block.** Chapter 1 has 3 (lines 51–55, 121–125, 377–446), Chapter 2 has 6, Chapter 6 has 8. Each block holds two authored versions (HEAD, then the other commit). Rule: for each block write one row in `ACTS.md` under "SOURCE CONFLICTS" (lines, what each side says, which you used, why). Default to the HEAD side where the sides disagree, because HEAD is the version aligned with the current course layer. Use text from the other side only when HEAD lacks a teaching point and the other side's version does not contradict anything else in the film. Blocks that differ only in an image filename, or that hold the "Preview of next chapter" and "AI Wayback Machine" end matter, are not teaching prose: say so in the row. State every real conflict in the final report so Bear can resolve it.
- **Chapter 1 carries Bear's uncommitted edits** (the "What I mean by skepticism" section, the Kantian slice, the solve-verify asymmetry, the five supervisory capacities, the fluency trap). Cover them. The chapter has known errors that earlier work found; treat each as a lead to verify against its primary, not as settled: the Knight Capital loss (chapter says $440M; the SEC order reports more than $460M), the turkey's day count (1,001), the Google Flu Trends "more than double", the sepsis model's 18% (of hospitalizations, not of patients), and "no errors were thrown". Earlier primaries are already fetched and logged in the FACTCHECK.md files of the show-tell films in the same `youtube/` folder (`show-tell-skepticism-is-a-method`, `show-tell-doubt-what-matters`, `show-tell-induction-until-it-changes`, `show-tell-a-test-that-cant-fail`, `show-tell-the-slice-you-can-see`, `show-tell-cheap-to-make-dear-to-check`, `show-tell-five-things-the-model-cant-do`, `show-tell-fluency-is-not-evidence`): reuse them, re-verify any number you speak, and correct the chapter's figure in the film, with a CORRECTED row. Every worked number is computed by a script and shown from its output (EXECUTABLE-EVIDENCE), including the chapter's triage illustration (0.30 / 0.76 ≈ 0.395), the 99%-accurate-test case, Bayes, and the calibration and heavy-tail examples.
