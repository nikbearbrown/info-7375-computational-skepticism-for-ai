(2) **Space, time, causality mind-supplied:** space and time as forms of sensible intuition (B xxv; A30/B45; A37/B54); cause and effect as a category of the understanding (A80/B106) and the Second Analogy (B232; A189/B234). (5) **Bear's asymptotic view:** EXEMPT, attributed to Bear aloud ("This is Bear's view, not Kant's") and on screen ("Bear's view"); never presented as Kant's or as settled. (6) **Gravity:** an illustration (EXEMPT); the only physics stated is that an apple falls and an orbit bends, with no numbers. Drawings, the greeting and the Your Turn prompt are EXEMPT.
