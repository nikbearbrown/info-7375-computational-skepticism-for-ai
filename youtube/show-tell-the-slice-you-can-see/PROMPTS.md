# PROMPTS — show-tell-the-slice-you-can-see

**What this is.** Every generation prompt. There are none: each image is a drawn isometric illustration in Manim (`scenes.py`, the ISO KIT). No stills, no clips, no photographs, no portrait of Kant or Hume, no Higgsfield spend. The only data-like drawing (B05's asymptote) is a schematic of Bear's stated view, not a measurement.

**Your Turn (BHTF)**, shown and read in full (spoken with "describe it" for `[describe it]`):

```
Here is one thing I, or a model I use, am confident about: [describe it]. Split it into three lists: what I can actually observe, what I am inferring from those observations, and one thing that lies outside my slice, which I have no way to observe at all. For each observed item, say how it was observed.
```

Checks shown under it (the viewer runs these themselves):
- Check: is every "observed" item really observed?
- Check: is the unobservable truly unobservable, not just unchecked?
