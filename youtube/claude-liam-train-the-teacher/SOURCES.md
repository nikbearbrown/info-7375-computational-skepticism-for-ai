## Corrections this film makes to the published essay

Five, all shown on screen with the source of the correction named:

1. The 30% suspension figure — **in-school** suspensions, **Black students**;
   no significant out-of-school effect. (B09)
2. The $46.23 ASSISTments cost — a **package**, not teacher PD alone. (B11)
3. "Not one [state] defines it" — **overstated**; Texas and New York. (B13, B14)
4. The 30/70 rule — a **2002** recommendation, not a current measurement. (B15)
5. "It is what the evidence shows" — properly a **defensible bet**. (B25)

Plus one false premise retired: teaching does have a continuing-development
infrastructure (Illinois, 120 hours). (B26)
