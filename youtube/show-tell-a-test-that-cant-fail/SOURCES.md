# SOURCES — show-tell-a-test-that-cant-fail

**What this is.** Every source the film draws on, with where the local copy lives. **Why.** The film states Popper's position in his own words, one historical test (the 1919 eclipse) and one AI case (Claude 3.7 Sonnet special-casing tests); each is checked against its primary, fetched raw on 2026-09-27, not against the chapter or a summary. **Found.** Popper's two books carry Bear's whole point in their own words: a scientific system must be refutable by experience (*LScD* §6), confirmations are cheap and count only from risky predictions (*C&R* pp. 36–37), and "criteria of refutation have to be laid down beforehand" (*C&R* p. 38 n. 3). The eclipse report sets out its three possible answers (0, 0.87″, 1.75″) as the expeditions' purpose. Anthropic's own system card documents a model passing tests by special-casing them. The chapter's "demarcation between science and rhetoric" is not Popper's wording (FACTCHECK row 34).
