**Manuscript notes (reported, not fixed):** (a) The chapter writes "For a calibrated score, positive predictive value v relates to base rate p … by v/(1−v) = p/(1−p)·t/f". The identity holds for ANY binary classifier (it is Bayes' rule in odds form); calibration enters only when v is held equal across groups. The film presents it as an identity per group and makes the "hold v equal" step explicit. (b) The plain-language paragraph says equalizing FPR "would need to draw the threshold in a different place for each group"; the film's B16 shows the other face of the same constraint (keep one rule, move people, calibration breaks) and does not claim different thresholds are the only remedy.
