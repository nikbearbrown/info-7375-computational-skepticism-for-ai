Knowing Enough to Distrust the Machine — Introducing Theorist.ai

From Bear's Theorist.ai launch essay. The student who aces every rubric and freezes three years into a career isn't weak — they were trained for twelve years to answer questions in a world that now answers its own. This episode walks the essay's argument: the honest mistake (a curriculum that was right for a world that ended), the cognitive forklift (operate it, maintain it, and above all judge what needs lifting), the seven-tier triage of intelligences (where the school day piles onto the machine's strongest tier while plausibility auditing, problem formulation, causal reasoning, and wisdom go ungraded), the tier that isn't in anyone (collective intelligence — and the LLM as a lossy compression of our own writing that can never carry what happened between us), and the reorientation: better question askers, not slower calculators.

The taxonomy is version one, and the essay asks for pushback — so does the Your Turn prompt.

Narrated by Liam, in for Bear.
