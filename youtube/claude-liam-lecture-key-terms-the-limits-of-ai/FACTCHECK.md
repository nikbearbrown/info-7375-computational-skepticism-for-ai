- Slide 4 step 3 caption 'The boundary moves outward and never reaches zero' mixes two quantities. The boundary shown is a length (1,680 units, 4 times the side) that grows as the sample grows. What shrinks without reaching zero is the excluded share (8.9%). The narration says the edge moves and is never removed, and does not repeat 'never reaches zero'.
- Slide 2: the caption and note say outside error 'stays near 0.86', but the red line and slides 11 and 12 show it rising from 0.73 to 0.86 as training points grow. 'Stays' understates that it gets worse.
- Slide 2 gives the inside error at 10 points as 0.285; slides 11 and 12 give the same value as 0.28 (0.285 would normally round to 0.29).
- Slide 1 map lists 'skepticism as method' and 'skepticism as safety' under the group 'Tests and arguments', but slide 8, where they are taught, carries the eyebrow 'Practice and delegation'.
- Evidence badges on slides 11 and 12 say 'Computed', but pair 3 (threshold 15, 1 of 6, 5 of 6), pair 4 and the last say-it-exactly row (5, 8 and 2 minutes) reuse numbers that slides 8 and 9 label illustrative, and the error and largest-draw figures come from seeded simulations.
